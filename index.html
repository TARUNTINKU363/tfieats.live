Our Story

Every Great Story Begins Somewhere.

TFI.Co was born from a shared passion for food and the unforgettable emotions of Telugu cinema.

We wanted to create more than just a food stall. We wanted to build a place where every waffle, every fry, and every customer becomes part of a memorable experience.

Cinema has the power to bring people together. Food does the same.

TFI.Co celebrates both. Every recipe is crafted with care, every serving is prepared fresh, and every visit is designed to feel like the first day of a blockbuster release.

Welcome to TFI.Co. Where every bite deserves a standing ovation.
